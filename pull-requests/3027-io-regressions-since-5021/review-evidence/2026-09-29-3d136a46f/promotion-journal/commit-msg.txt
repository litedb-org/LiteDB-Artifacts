Prove no promotion starts behind an earlier header journal

The PR listed "a promotion refused while an earlier header journal is
outstanding" as untested, with a legacy conversion journal kept at open
as its precondition. That state is not reachable in an engine that has
not failed: an open retires every journal it recovers before it accepts
a write, or does not open writable. The one it keeps, a legacy
conversion journal whose converted header never synced, needs the index
migration, whose drain backfills behind it and empties the log before
the conversion journals its own header; a conversion whose header does
not sync opens read-only before the migration's promotion.

A test-only observer (EngineState.ObservePromotion, registered in
fault-points.json) reports each promotion as it starts under the WAL
writer and whether a journal is outstanding. KeptJournalPromotion_Tests,
plain and encrypted, builds the kept conversion journal from the 5.0.21
fixture, reopens it while the data file cannot sync (refused before any
promotion, log and power-loss image unchanged), then with syncing
storage refuses the migration's promotion at its start (both files
unchanged, no failure recorded, every row after a power loss) and
finally migrates; a promotion journal an open repairs a torn header from
is retired before the next promotion.
