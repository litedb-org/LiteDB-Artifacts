Prove four more #3027 fixes against the published 6.0.0-prerelease.319

A PR fixing several bugs adds one regression proof per bug. Register four
more, each a black-box ReproRunner repro pinned to LiteDB
6.0.0-prerelease.319, the published NuGet package built from dev at 5dd942a7
(this PR's base):

- Issue_3027_DumpInTransaction: after $dump(pageID) or $page_list(pageID)
  read pages an explicit transaction (Transaction Pages=50) had safepointed
  into the WAL, its next safepoint fails an ENSURE (LiteException 999 "only
  idle readable pages can be evicted"), the engine stops, the header is
  marked invalid and the transaction is lost. Guard: DumpPinnedWalSlot_Tests.
- Issue_3027_StreamDatabaseDispose: new LiteDatabase(stream) leaves
  CHECKPOINT=1 in the header of a file used without Dispose, a second Dispose
  throws "This engine instance already disposed." and Dispose with an open
  transaction throws before disposing the engine. Guard:
  StreamDatabaseDispose_Tests.
- Issue_3027_LegacyReadOnlyStream: new LiteDatabase(stream) over a read-only
  FileStream of the 5.0.21 DropIndex customers.db throws
  NotSupportedException; beside a writable log stream it first writes
  32768 bytes into the log. Guard: LegacyReadOnlyStream_Tests.
- Issue_3027_ForeignLegacyWal: the 5.0.21 ForeignWal log beside WalCrash's
  crash.db is replayed into it (40960 to 14196736 bytes, log deleted,
  collections big and fresh in place of docs). Guards: LegacyWalPageBound_Tests,
  ConvertedWalBesideLegacyHeader_Tests and the 5.0.21 compatibility script.
  This variant was chosen because its known-bad damage is bounded by the
  fixture (pages up to 1732). The repro checks the fixture digests and caps
  its files at 64 MiB with RLIMIT_FSIZE (SIGXFSZ ignored), so a far page fails
  with EFBIG and cannot grow a sparse file.

Each repro exits 0 only on its defect, 1 only after verifying the fixed
behavior in full and 2 otherwise; repro.json pins both exit codes and log
texts. The candidate opens, commits and reads back as the guards require.
All four also reproduce against dev-commit 5dd942a7 packed with
pack-known-bad. The repros are retired from the automatic Repro Runner tiers
like the earlier proofs, and the compose-matrix counts follow.
