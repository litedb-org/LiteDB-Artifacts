Sync and write nothing in a promotion that waited on a failed checkpoint

A file format promotion takes the header lock and then the WAL writer,
and looked neither at the engine's stop nor at a recorded failure. A
checkpoint that fails under the WAL writer records the failure (an I/O
error) and begins the stop there, then releases its locks before the
teardown. A compact insert that had passed its checks and waited for the
header lock (the checkpoint's commit lock) then promoted the v11 file to
v12 in that window: it synced the data file again on the handle whose
sync had just failed (fsyncgate: the retry "succeeds"), wrote its header
journal into the log and the new header into the data file, five syncs
after the failure. Decision 6 says nothing more is written or synced once
a failure is recorded, also by an operation that passed its entry check.

The promotion now validates the engine and requires no recorded failure
under the WAL writer, as a WAL batch does. CheckpointFailureWindow_Tests
forces the window through the checkpoint's coordination signal: an EIO
data sync (caught by the record check and the stop), the same with the
stop deferred past the locks (only the record check catches it) and a
failure that is no I/O error (only the stop catches it); each guard
removed fails its row. Found by the review of the refused-promotion path
(owner's review of 4662fe49f, item 4).
